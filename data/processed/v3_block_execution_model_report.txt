Traffic block, power block and disconnection are modeled as operational restrictions.
Integrated and shadow blocks are modeled as coordination or opportunity arrangements that still retain all underlying safety, resource and authorization requirements.

TrackEase recommends and validates candidate arrangements; it does not grant or authorize a railway block.
